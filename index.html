The strategy
Two campaigns, one goal

Running both sub-campaigns at the same time covers the full funnel — the first locks in your existing listeners, the second brings in new ones. Together they give YoYo the best shot at tipping over.

Sub-Campaign 1
$100
Your Fans First
Targets people who already have a relationship with your music — listeners who've gone quiet for 28+ days. You can also select Deepen fan engagement as an optional third goal to bring in fans who are still actively listening right now.

Potential listeners
The most important thing for this campaign: leave Potential listeners unchecked. That single setting is what keeps this spend targeted to your existing fan base only. Everything else is flexible.
Spotify may flag this as "Not likely to deliver" — that's expected. The audience is intentionally narrow. Campaign 2 covers the broader reach.

Everything Campaign 1 does, plus it adds potential new listeners — people who don't know your music yet but whose listening habits match your audience. The bigger pool means better delivery likelihood and more new streams.
